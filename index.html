The Botanical Ritual

Part dry hair into sections. Apply 3–5 drops along parts and gently massage into scalp with fingertips for 5 minutes. Leave on for at least 30 minutes before washing.

Warm 1–2 drops between palms and work through damp or dry ends to lock in moisture, eliminate frizz, and shield against environmental stressors.

Apply generously to scalp and lengths. Wrap hair in a silk scarf overnight and cleanse thoroughly in the morning for deep restorative nourishment.
